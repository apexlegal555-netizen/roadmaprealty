Chapter 1

⚠️ The Problem — India's Real Estate Trust Crisis

Think of it like this...

Imagine buying a toy that doesn't work. Now imagine that toy costs your life savings. That's property buying in India today.

Four interconnected problems:

1

70% Unverified Listings

7 out of 10 property listings lack proper RERA registration, title verification, or encumbrance clearance. No recourse mechanism exists for buyers.

2

2M+ Untrained Brokers

Over 2 million individual brokers operate without standardized inspection processes or audit trails.

3

High Transaction Risk

Property documentation fraud and misrepresentation cause major buyer losses with limited legal remedies.

4

Tier-2/3 City Gap

Smaller cities lack professional verification, forcing buyers to rely on unverified local information.

Think of it like this...

Like a 5-star hotel rating system, but for houses. Every property goes through a rigorous certification process before earning its certificate.

Chapter 3

🔧 How It Works — The Complete Verification Flow

Step 1

📝 Property Listed

Seller lists property through our franchise office or partner portal.

Step 2

📄 Document Collection

Seller provides: sale deed, title chain (30 years), encumbrance certificate, RERA registration, tax receipts, approved plan.

Step 3

🔍 Physical Inspection

Trained inspector visits property: GPS entry photo, 6 exterior + 8 interior photos, 30s walkthrough video, room measurements, red-flag checklist.

Step 4

📄 Government Record Check

RERA portal verification (API/manual) + land records check (Mahabhulekh/Bhoomi/Dharani per state) + lawyer legal review within 48 hours.

Step 5

🏆 QR Certificate Issued

Unique certificate ID with QR code. Scannable to view all inspection data, documents, and verification status. Valid 6 months.

Step 6

🏦 Escrow-Protected Transaction

Buyer's funds held in bank escrow account. Released to seller only after document transfer and legal sign-off. AML/KYC verified.

⚖️ Regulatory & Compliance Framework

Think of it like this...

Building a house without a foundation is dangerous. Similarly, our business needs a strong legal and regulatory foundation to survive and thrive.

1. Escrow Compliance (RBI Guidelines)

Critical: Escrow is the highest-risk activity

We are not a licensed escrow agent. We use a bank's Escrow-as-a-Service (EaaS) model through a scheduled commercial bank. This is different from RERA's 70% escrow for developer projects — our escrow is for buyer-seller transactions.

RequirementStatusTimeline
RBI-compliant escrow through scheduled commercial bankIn progressWeek 0-4 (Phase 1)
Documented release triggers (legal sign-off, document transfer)In progressWeek 0-4
AML/KYC compliance proceduresTo buildWeek 4-8
Independent trustee / monthly auditTo set upPhase 2
Legal opinion from counsel confirming RBI compliance across pilot statesTop priorityWeek 0-2

2. Title Verification — Beyond RERA (Critical Gap)

RERA covers new developer projects only. The secondary market (where highest fraud occurs) requires:

  • 30-year title chain: Tracing ownership back through sale deeds, inheritance, and mutation records.
  • Encumbrance certificates (EC): Checking for existing loans, liens, or legal claims on the property.
  • Village survey maps: Each state has its own land record system (7/12 in Maharashtra, RTC in Karnataka, Dharani in Telangana).
  • Approved building plan verification: Checking against municipal corporation records for illegal construction.

3. DPDP Act 2023 Compliance

We collect highly sensitive data: Aadhaar, PAN, sale deeds, financial data, GPS location, property photos/videos.

  • Data localization within Indian servers
  • Explicit consent collection per data category
  • Certificate data archived 7 years, personal data purged after 3 years
  • Right to erasure available after retention period
  • Breach notification within 72 hours

4. Franchise Agreement Compliance

  • Indian Contract Act, 1872 registration
  • Trademark licensing under Trade Marks Act, 1999
  • GST on franchise fees (18% under SAC 997212)
  • Stamp duty varies by state

5. Insurance Tie-Up Regulatory Approval

  • Referral model (not direct selling) does not require IRDAI license
  • Must sign referral agreement with IRDAI-licensed insurer
  • Revenue share within IRDAI-prescribed limits

Like a doctor's diagnostic checklist — follow it step by step, and you never miss anything critical.

30-Year Title Audit SOP

1

Collect Title Documents

Request: chain of sale deeds (30 years), mother deed, partition deeds, gift deeds, will/succession documents.

2

Verify Encumbrance Certificate

Obtain EC from sub-registrar office for last 30 years. Check for mortgages, liens, or pending litigation.

3

Cross-Check Land Records

State-specific: 7/12 extract (MH), RTC (KA), Patta Chitta (TN), Dharani (TS). Verify owner name matches title deed.

4

Check Mutation Entries

Verify property mutated to current owner's name in municipal/revenue records.

5

RERA Verification

For new projects: check RERA registration number, project status, completion timeline.

6

Red-Flag Checklist (Forced Fields)

Title chain gap, existing encumbrances, pending dues, illegal construction, occupant disputes, multiple ownership claims, power of attorney concerns.
